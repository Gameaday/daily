How to play

Every morning you get a short passage from history. Every phrase in it is true — except one, which is bogus: an anachronism, an invented detail, or a plain mistake.

Click the phrase you think is the lie. A wrong pick turns red and costs one point. Score is 3 minus your wrong picks — a first-pick find is a perfect 3/3. Find the lie and it turns green, with the real story behind the fib.

Three wrong picks and the day is lost (you still get the reveal — it's the best part). Win and your streak grows. Miss a day and the streak resets.

Everyone in the world gets the same puzzle, so compare picks at standup — argue before you click.
