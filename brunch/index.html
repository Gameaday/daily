How to play

One city and one year from the record. Three of the four names hold a documented stay in that city that year; one was elsewhere. Pick the one who missed the table.

The reveal shows where everyone was, with the years the record states. Presence is documented conservatively, so a name that belongs at the table never shows up as the impostor.

One guess a day. Everyone gets the same table, so compare notes over coffee.
