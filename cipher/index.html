How to play

Every letter of the quote has been swapped for another letter, consistently. The small letter on top is the cipher; your decode goes underneath.

Tap an encrypted letter to select it (every copy of it highlights), then type your guess for it. Backspace clears it. The author is shown in the clear — use the spelling as a crib.

Nothing is marked while you type, and a wrong decode just looks wrong. Press Check when you think you have it: it tells you how many letters are right (never which ones), and solves the day the moment your decode is complete and correct.

Stuck? A hint reveals one letter (the most common one you are missing or have wrong) — the day's score is how many hints you needed. Reveal the codebook ends the day.
