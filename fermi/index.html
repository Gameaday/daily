How to play

One estimation question a day, one guess. Type your estimate in whatever format is comfortable — raw digits, 2.5b, 3e8, or "2.5 billion".

You are scored by how close you land, on a ratio scale:

3 / 3within 3× of the cited value
2 / 3within 10×
1 / 3within 100×
0 / 3beyond that — the gut needs calibration

"Within an order of magnitude" (2/3 or better) keeps your streak alive. The trick is not precision — it's getting the size of the thing right. Everyone gets the same question, so compare gut checks at standup.
