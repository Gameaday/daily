How to play

Each clue is the list of runs of filled cells in that line, in order. A clue of 3 1 means three filled, a gap, then one filled — somewhere in the seven cells. A clue of 0 means the whole line is empty.

Tap a cell to fill it, tap again to mark it ✕ (known-empty), once more to clear. When your grid matches the clues, hit Develop.

If the picture is wrong, that costs one of your four developments: wrongly-filled cells flash red, and cells you left blank flash dashed. Fix and develop again. Four misses and the picture is revealed — fogged.

Every day develops into a little piece of history. The clock starts on your first tap and never restarts — it's for bragging, not scoring.
