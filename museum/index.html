How to play

Each day one artifact from world history is hiding behind five clues, ordered from obscure to obvious.

Type a guess at any point. Right — the exhibit lights up. Wrong — you get the next clue, and the score is how many clues you needed.

Another clue takes the next clue without guessing; after the fifth clue one more wrong guess ends the visit.

Naming it on the first clue is the perfect run. The rest of us call it a good morning.
