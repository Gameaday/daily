How to play

Use the arrow buttons around the grid to shift a row or a column one step. Letters wrap around the edge. Every real word of 3+ letters reading left-to-right in a row or top-to-bottom in a column counts.

Three modes, three budgets: Practice · 4 — a fresh random board every time, validated instantly, nothing recorded. Normal · 5 — the daily board; your one bank counts for your streak and the global curve. Hard · 6 — the same daily board with one more shift, online only, posting to its own bonus curve.

One shared budget: every shift — row or column — spends one of your shifts, in any order you like. All twenty arrows are live from the first tap; there are no phases and no separate pools.

Undo takes back your last shift and refunds its cost. Restart returns the board to its start with the budget untouched. Both are free — explore!

Best possible is exact: while you play, the page walks every board the rules can reach within your budget — any mix of rows and columns, in any order — so the number is a fact, not an estimate. It stays hidden while a counted game is live (practice shows it as the target) and appears with your result when you bank. The walk lands within your first few shifts; a bank before it lands is judged against the always-ready row-then-column optimum, which free play can only exceed.

Your best board is stashed: keep exploring past a good board, and Return to best jumps straight back to the most words you have seen. Undo already refunds the budget; the stash just saves you the walk.

Bank It locks in the words currently on the board. In Normal the first bank of the day counts; Hard banks its own bonus curve; Practice records nothing.

Same letters for everyone in Normal and Hard, so compare banks at standup.
