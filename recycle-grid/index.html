How to play

Use the arrow buttons around the grid to shift any row or column one step. Letters wrap around the edge.

Every real word of 3 or more letters that appears reading left-to-right in a row, or top-to-bottom in a column, counts. The chips below the grid show everything currently on the board.

The recycle rule: the clock starts on your first shift and never restarts — not for the Recycle button, not for reloading. The Recycle button returns the grid to its original layout (clock keeps running) so you can try a totally different plan.

Bank It locks in your score: the number of words on the board at that moment. More words = better. One bank per day, so don't get greedy forever — but every extra shift costs time, and your time is the tiebreaker.

Same letters for everyone, so compare banks at standup.
