How to play

Use the arrow buttons around the grid to shift a row or a column one step. Letters wrap around the edge. Every real word of 3+ letters reading left-to-right in a row or top-to-bottom in a column counts.

Three modes, three budgets: Practice · 4 — a fresh random board every time, validated instantly, nothing recorded. Normal · 5 — the daily board; your one bank counts for your streak and the team curve. Hard · 6 — the same daily board with one more shift, online only (the exact search is the heavy one), posting to its own bonus curve.

Two phases: first you may only shift rows. Press Lock rows when you're happy with them (free) and the columns unlock — one-way, no going back to rows.

Undo takes back your last shift and refunds its cost. Restart returns the board to its start with the budget untouched. Both are free — explore!

Best possible is exact: before you play, the page has already tried every shift plan within your budget, so the "best possible" number is a fact. Match it and Bank It for a perfect bank.

Bank It locks in the words currently on the board. In Normal the first bank of the day counts; Hard banks its own bonus curve; Practice records nothing.

Same letters for everyone in Normal and Hard, so compare banks at standup.
