How to play

Four wheels of letters. The marked ray at the top reads one letter from each wheel, and together they must spell today's target word. The word sits above the wheel, with your current letters at the ray beneath it.

Spin any wheel with its ◀ ▶ buttons to rotate that wheel alone. Shift the ray with the ◀ ▶ buttons at the end: every letter at the ray slides one seat together, the whole column at once.

Par is exact: before you play, the solver has already searched every possible wheel state for the shortest line to the word. Beat par and you found a line the solver missed (you didn't — it's exact).

There is no clock. Every spin and every swap costs one move, and reversing a move costs another — so plan before you spin.

Same wheels for everyone, so compare move counts at standup.
