How to play

Three wheels of letters, one marked ray at the top. Spin any wheel with its ◀ ▶ buttons to bring the right letters up to the ray.

When all three slots spell today's word, you win — the clock stops.

The recycle rule: the clock starts on your first spin and never restarts — not for retries, not for reloading the page, not for the Recycle button. Your time is your score, so think before you spin. Par is the theoretical minimum number of spins; chase it if you dare.

Everyone in the world gets the same wheels, so compare times at standup.
