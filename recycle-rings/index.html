How to play

Four rings of eight letters, sitting in eight rays, and opposite rays are one straight line of eight letters through the center. The marked ray at the top reads one letter from each ring, and together they must spell today's target word. The word sits above the rings, with your current letters at the ray beneath it.

Loop any ring: its ◀ ▶ buttons turn it one seat, or drag any of its letters around the wheel and it follows your finger, snapping to the nearest seat when you let go. Shift a line with the outward chevron at either end, or by dragging any of its letters along its spoke: the whole line of eight letters slides one seat together, and the letter pushed off the end wraps around to the far end.

The wheel answers in color: a letter sitting in the ray that does not spell its seat turns red, and the word's own letters waiting out in the wrong place turn gold. A letter home at the ray stays green. So even a board where nothing fits yet tells you the whole story: the red seats are the destinations, the gold letters are the freight, and matching them up is the line.

Resources, not moves: taking up a seat costs one resource, however far you run it. Keep turning the same ring, or keep sliding the same line - either end, back and forth, wraps and all - and it costs nothing more; switch to another seat, or come back to one you left, and it costs again. So think laterally: a long sweep of one line can be cheaper than a ladder of little corrections.

Par is exact: the solver has already walked every reachable position of the four word letters, counting resources the same way, so par is the fewest the day can be solved in. Beat par and you found a line the solver missed (you didn't; it's exact).

The bonus round: win the day and the same board opens again under the step economy, where every seat a move covers costs and par is a count of steps. A different economy, often a different number. It posts to its own little curve, and no streak rides on it.

Same rings for everyone, so compare resources at standup.
