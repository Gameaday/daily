How to play

Four rings of eight letters, sitting in eight aligned rays. The marked ray at the top reads one letter from each ring, and together they must spell today's target word. The word sits above the rings, with your current letters at the ray beneath it.

Loop any ring: its ◀ ▶ buttons turn it one seat, or drag any of its letters around the wheel and it follows your finger, snapping to the nearest seat when you let go. Shift a ray with the chevrons at its outer end, or by dragging any of its letters along its spoke: all four letters slide one ring inward or outward together, wrapping around, so the inner ring hands its letter to the outer ring.

Par is exact: before you play, the solver has already searched every reachable position of the four word letters for the shortest line to the word. Beat par and you found a line the solver missed (you didn't; it's exact).

There is no clock. Every loop and every ray shift costs one move, a drag simply charges one move per seat it covers, and reversing costs another, so plan before you slide.

Same rings for everyone, so compare move counts at standup.
