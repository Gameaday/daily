How to play

Each day seats three lives from the people record. Every round asks the same kind of question: which of two events happened nearer the life's birth, or nearer its death. Both events fall inside the life, and their years sit close enough that the answer is a judgement, never a giveaway.

The record settles each round itself: one event sits so many years after the birth, or before the death, and the nearer one answers the question. The fine print lays out both distances, so even a wrong pick teaches you the arithmetic.

A life can return across the season, but never on consecutive days and never too often. A clean sweep, all three rounds right, keeps your streak alive.

One board a day. Everyone gets the same session, so compare notes over coffee.
