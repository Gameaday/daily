How to play

Slide to the year you think it happened and Lock it in, or nudge it one year at a time with the - and + buttons, or just type the year into the box. You get six rounds. Each lock tells you how close you landed, and which direction to go:

🟩 scalding — within 2 years
🟨 hot — within 5
🟧 warm — within 15
🟥 cold — further out

The row tells you "go earlier" or "go later", plus a generous nudge like "wrong century" or "right decade", but never the distance. Hit the exact year and the round is yours. Six misses and the year is revealed.

Fewer rounds, more bragging rights. Everyone gets the same event, so compare rounds at standup.
