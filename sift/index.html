How to play

Tap tiles to select up to three, then hit Test selection. A correct trio locks in, reveals its category name, and gets out of your way.

A wrong trio costs a mistake. You can afford four; a fifth… you can't. If you run out, the remaining groups are revealed and the day is over.

The categories are easier than they look — but at least one tile in every puzzle is a triple threat: it plausibly belongs in two or three groups. Trust the grouping that's exactly three tiles, not the one that's "mostly right."

Everyone plays the same board, same order, every day. Compare miss counts at standup.
