    How to play

    Each round shows five scrambled words — one 4-letter, then four 5-letter. Type an anagram and hit enter: any dictionary word using exactly those letters scores, so if we scrambled STONE and you spot TONES first, take it.

    Skip parks a word and moves on. Circle back? Only if you clear the rest with time to spare. (You won't. You never do.) Staring at the same letters for twenty seconds earns a Shuffle: same word, fresh arrangement, no time cost.

    The 90-second clock starts on your first keystroke. Reload the page, switch tabs, close your laptop — the clock keeps running. That's the sprint.
