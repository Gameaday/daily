How to play

Four lives from the people record, one question: who lived the longest, or who was born first or last. Pick the one the record makes stand alone.

The record's spans arrive once the bonus match is settled. A life only counts when the record dates both ends, and a group where the answer could tie never serves, so the one to pick is always settled alone.

Some boards carry a bonus match: four superlatives, one for each life. Pick a superlative, then pick the life it belongs to. Every life tops the one it holds.

One guess a day. Everyone gets the same board, so compare notes over coffee.
