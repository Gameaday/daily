How to play

Each day seats a cohort of four or five lives from the people record, then asks a run of questions about them, one at a time: pick the life each superlative fits. The same life can answer more than one question, and the record never serves a question a tie could settle, so every round has exactly one right answer.

The questions read the record's own numbers: the longest or shortest life, the first or last birth, the birth nearest the cohort's middle, the most average life. A measured question, the longest or shortest reign, the most voyages, the most or fewest works, or height, only counts the lives a source actually measured, at least two, never a tie.

A life can come back in a later cohort, but never with a majority of the same company, so returning faces arrive with new neighbors and the days stay fresh. A clean sweep, every round right, keeps your streak alive.

One cohort a day. Everyone gets the same session, so compare notes over coffee.
