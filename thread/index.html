How to play

A start and an end stand fixed at the top and bottom. The tray holds every figure on the one true chain between them, plus two decoys that belong nowhere on it.

Drag a card from the tray into any slot, or tap a card and then a slot. Drag a placed card back to the tray, or onto another slot to swap the two.

Check the chain counts as one check and locks every figure that sits in its true place, so each check shrinks the puzzle.

The score is how few checks you needed. A perfect read of history is one.

Give up ends the day and lays the chain out properly.
