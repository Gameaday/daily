How to play

Five events appear in a random order with no dates. Drag a card to a new slot, or tap two cards to swap them.

Check order counts as one check and tells you how many cards are in the right place — correct ones lock and show their year, so each check shrinks the problem.

The score is how few checks you needed. A perfect read of history is one.

Give up ends the day and reveals the years, laid out properly.
