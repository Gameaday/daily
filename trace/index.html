How to play

Type a word that differs from your current word by exactly one letter (like COLD → CORD). Every rung must be a real word — the game knows the dictionary, so guess in good faith. You can’t reuse words you’ve already climbed.

Reach the target word and the ladder is solved. Your score is the number of rungs you used — the fewer the better. Par is the shortest ladder that exists (the game checked, so you don’t have to wonder).

Stuck? Reveal a path ends the climb and shows one shortest solution.

There\u2019s no mistake limit — the score is efficiency, not survival. The clock starts on your first climb and never restarts; it\u2019s just for bragging.
