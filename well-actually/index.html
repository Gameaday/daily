How to play

Every morning you get one statement. One word or phrase in it is wrong — the rest is true.

Click the phrase you think is the error. A wrong pick turns red and costs one of your three picks.

Find the error and it turns green — then you get one attempt to type what it should say instead. Score is 3 minus your wrong picks, plus 1 for a correct correction — a first-pick find with the bonus nailed is a perfect 4-point morning.

Everyone in the world gets the same puzzle, so compare scores at standup — argue before you click.
